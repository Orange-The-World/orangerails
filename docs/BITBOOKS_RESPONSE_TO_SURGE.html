1 Reception

Three things in your plan stand out as well-designed and rare for a v1 Partner API.

  • The auth model is right. One-time biometric signature in the user's pocket, opaque base64 token, server-side revocation list. We store ciphertext, never see the EVM key, never custody anything. It composes cleanly with our zero-knowledge architecture — we encrypt the token alongside everything else under the customer's vault key.
  • /accounting-state is the killer endpoint. Returning Bitcoin, Pledged to Surge and Surge Line of Credit as named buckets means our consumer side is JSON-to-journal-entry, not a calculation engine. We plan to adopt those exact strings as our default chart-of-accounts names so the customer sees the same label in both apps.
  • Stable error codes. Switching on code instead of HTTP status alone is how we want to handle the 401 → re-prompt flow. We mapped every code in §14 of your spec.

5 Questions to lock before production

Three buckets: Blocker needs answering before we can finish Phase 1, Clarify is a documentation gap we'd like nailed down, Future is on the roadmap for later phases.

#TypeQuestionWhy it matters to us
Q1BlockerLock the exact token wire format (§5 in your plan says TBD): JSON field names, signature scheme (personal_sign vs EIP-712), version byte, max length.Determines the paste-and-store UI on our side, plus the max column width on the encrypted credential record.
Q2BlockerFinalize the /partner/me response shape (§12 also TBD).We call this immediately after a paste to verify the token works before the UI flips to "Connected."
Q3BlockerShould the auth envelope also require a BitBooks-issued partner secret alongside the user's signature (your open #1)?We strongly recommend yes — defense-in-depth. A leaked user token alone should not be exploitable without our partner credential. Adds one onboarding step on your side.
Q4ClarifyConfirm the amount basis convention. Your /positions example shows principal_usdc: "250" alongside principal_usdc_amount: 0.00025. Is "250" atomic units at 6 decimals?An off-by-six-decimals interpretation would mis-state every loan.
Q5ClarifyFull enum of status values. Docs only show "active". Are the others closed, liquidated, defaulted, repaid?Each maps to a different account state on the customer's books.
Q6ClarifyDoes interest capitalize into principal, or stay separately tracked? Today accrued_interest_usdc is null on every position.Capitalization changes how every repaid event splits between principal and interest accounts.
Q7ClarifyIs the "60 req/min per partner per owner" rate limit per-owner (good) or aggregate per partner (constraining)? Confirm Retry-After semantics.Sets our polling cadence and concurrency model for multi-customer batch syncs.
Q8ClarifyRecommended polling cadence per owner.Drives infrastructure cost and UX freshness on our side.
Q9ClarifyRevocation propagation — when a user revokes in the Surge app, is rejection immediate or eventually consistent across your edge?Determines whether a transient 401 on our side should mean "re-prompt user" or "retry once."
Q10FutureAdd liquidation to the event action vocabulary, with proceeds-at-block in the payload.Without it, a liquidation only shows as a balance delta with no journal trail. Liquidation is a tax-realization event — we must capture realized gain/loss versus cost basis.
Q11FutureWebhook on revocation (push, not poll).Lets us mark the connection inactive in the customer's UI without waiting for the next poll to 401.
Q12FutureTimeline for accrued_interest_now and accrued_interest_at(timestamp) from your "further planned" section.Today we have to estimate accruals client-side and true them up later. Earliest your team can promise this matters a lot for our period-end story.
Q13ClarifyVersioning + deprecation policy for /api/v1.Helps us scope when to invest in v2-readiness on our side.
Q14ClarifyNaming: your doc says "BitBooks · OrangeRails" throughout. As of our 2026-05-14 product split, Orange Rails is a distinct product under the BitBooks umbrella. We suggest "Orange Rails (by BitBooks)" in customer-facing copy.Cosmetic, but helps both brand stories.
